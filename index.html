About Me

I am a Public Health student expected to graduate in 2028. I chose public health because I have always loved science and medicine, but I wanted to make a broader impact on the health and well-being of communities.


The health issue I care most about is workplace safety because I have experienced workplace hazards in my field. These experiences have motivated me to learn more about protecting workers and promoting healthier work environments.


My career goal is to work in occupational health, where I can help improve workplace conditions and support the safety and health of workers.
